## 🌟 Why Kids Tracker?

In today's fast-paced world, the safety of children is every parent's top priority. Whether it's the daily school commute, crowded amusement parks, or weekend family outings, unexpected separation can happen in a split second.

**Kids Tracker** is a commercial-grade, highly reliable, and privacy-secure smart anti-lost and location-tracking system built for modern families. It eliminates the common pain points of traditional tracking apps—such as frequent disconnection, low accuracy, and lack of emergency response—by utilizing a robust multi-module architecture, advanced security mechanisms, and instant emergency linkage.

---

## 💎 Five Core Commercial Highlights

### 1. 🛰️ Millisecond High-Precision Positioning & 5s Live Polling
* **AMap 3D Engine**: Seamlessly integrated with the AMap SDK to deliver precise indoor/outdoor geolocation and reverse geocoding, converting raw coordinates into intuitive, readable street addresses.
* **5-Second Auto Polling**: Once paired, the parent app automatically fetches the child's latest location every 5 seconds in the background. Watch location updates happen in real-time as your child moves without manual refreshing.

### 2. 🚨 Smart Safe Distance Fence & Instant Emergency Linkage
* **Customizable Safe Radius**: Parents can freely set safe distance thresholds at 50m, 100m, 200m, 500m, or 1000m.
* **Vibration & Ringtone Dual Alert**: When the straight-line distance between parent and child exceeds the set safe radius, the parent's phone instantly triggers strong vibration and an alarm sound, accompanied by a high-visibility red warning banner.
* **One-Tap Manual Silence**: Once triggered, parents can silence the alarm and vibration anytime with a single tap.
* **Auto-Call, SMS & Audio Evidence**:
  - **Auto-Call**: Instantly calls the parent's phone upon safe boundary breach.
  - **Auto-SMS**: Automatically sends a warning SMS containing the child's precise location address.
  - **Auto-Recording**: Automatically starts a 30-second environmental audio recording upon boundary breach as critical acoustic evidence.

### 3. 🔐 Industry-First 3-Layer Security & Privacy Shield
* **Dynamic 10-Minute OTP**: The 6-digit pairing code generated on the child's device automatically expires after 10 minutes and rotates, completely eliminating the risk of static pairing code leakage or brute-force tracking.
* **Parent Account & JWT Authentication**: Built with a modern account system and secure Token authentication, ensuring all network requests are encrypted and verified.
* **Multi-Tenant Authorization Isolation**: Implements a "Master Guardian" and family sharing model. Unauthorized third parties are strictly blocked with `403 Forbidden` responses, guaranteeing absolute family privacy.

### 4. 🔋 Robust Anti-Uninstall & Background Guardian Service
* **Android 14+ Deep Adaptation**: Fully compatible with the latest foreground service requirements, ensuring continuous GPS data collection even when running in the background or when the screen is locked.
* **Anti-Uninstall Protection**: Supports Device Administrator and Accessibility Service plugins to prevent accidental deletion or forced termination by children.
